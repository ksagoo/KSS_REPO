**Reason for Requested Change**

Request to increase the **GIN server token expiry period from 14 days to 31 days**.

ITSOs are required to plan and schedule certificate renewals up to one month in advance. The current 14-day token validity period means approval tokens can expire before the scheduled renewal date, resulting in failed approvals when ITSOs click **APPROVE**.

Extending the token validity to 31 days will align with the certificate renewal planning window, ensuring approval links remain valid and preventing unnecessary delays to certificate renewals.
